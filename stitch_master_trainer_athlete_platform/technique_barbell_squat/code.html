Barbell Squat

The foundational compound movement for lower body strength and power.

play_arrow
signal_cellular_alt_1_bar Beginner
fitness_center Barbell
repeat 3-4 Sets, 8-12 Reps

bolt What This Does

The barbell squat primarily targets the quadriceps, glutes, and hamstrings while demanding immense core stability and lower back strength. It stimulates central nervous system adaptation, leading to comprehensive lower body power and structural integrity.

format_list_numbered Step-by-Step

  • 1

    Setup: Position bar across upper back (traps/rear delts). Unrack, take 2 steps back.

  • 2

    Descent: Break at hips and knees simultaneously. Lower until thighs are parallel to floor.

  • 3

    Ascent: Drive through mid-foot, keeping chest up and knees tracking out. Return to start.

Pro Coaching Tip

"Imagine spreading the floor apart with your feet as you stand up. This forcefully engages the gluteus medius and prevents knee valgus."

warning Common Mistakes

  • close Knees caving inward (valgus collapse) during ascent.
  • close Losing lower back neutrality (butt wink) at the bottom.
  • close Heels raising off the ground due to poor ankle mobility.
health_and_safety

Safety Notice

Always perform heavy squats inside a power rack with safety pins set at appropriate heights. If you experience sharp pain, terminate the set immediately. Master form with light weight before progressing.
